Privacy Policy

Quiet Reef Runner — published by Ahaay Games
Effective date: 18 July 2026
Contact: ahaaygames@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains what information the mobile game Quiet Reef Runner ("the game", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By playing the game you agree to the practices described here.

1. Summary

  • The game is playable offline. Core gameplay and your progress are stored on your device.
  • We show ads through Google AdMob, which may collect device and advertising identifiers.
  • Optional in-app purchases (e.g. "Remove Ads", "Supporter Pack") are processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment card details.
  • An online leaderboard uses Unity Gaming Services. It stores a display nickname you choose and your best score/time.
  • We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or precise location.

2. Information we collect

a. Information collected automatically for ads and analytics. When ads are shown, our advertising partner (Google AdMob) may collect and process device information such as a mobile advertising identifier, IP address, device type and OS version, and general interaction data. This is used to serve and measure ads. See Google's policies below.

b. Purchase information. If you buy an in-app product, the transaction is handled by Google Play Billing. The game receives only your entitlement status (for example, whether "Remove Ads" is owned) — not your card number or billing address.

c. Online features (leaderboard). To take part in the leaderboard, the game uses Unity Gaming Services Authentication to create an anonymous player identifier (no email or account login is required) and stores the nickname you choose together with your best score and time. Your chosen nickname is public on the leaderboard, so please do not use personal information as your nickname.

d. On-device data. Game progress, coins, unlocked items, best results, and settings are saved locally on your device and are removed when you uninstall the game.

3. How we use information

  • To run and improve core gameplay and save your progress.
  • To display and measure advertising, and to offer a rewarded "Continue".
  • To process in-app purchases and restore them.
  • To operate the cross-platform leaderboard and show rankings.

4. Advertising and consent

We use Google AdMob to display interstitial and rewarded ads. Where required by law (for example, for users in the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland), the game presents a consent choice using Google's User Messaging Platform before requesting personalized ads. You can decline personalized ads; the game remains fully playable. You can also reset advertising identifiers in your device settings.

5. Third-party services

The following third parties may process data as described in their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (advertising) — policies.google.com/privacy and AdMob data disclosure.
  • Google Play Billing (purchases) — policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Unity Gaming Services (authentication & leaderboards) — unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the service providers listed above to the extent needed to run the features you use, or where required by law.

7. Data retention

On-device data remains until you clear it or uninstall the game. Leaderboard entries (nickname and best result) are retained while the leaderboard is active. Advertising and purchase data are retained by Google/Unity according to their own policies.

8. Children's privacy

The game is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in a way that requires parental consent. If you believe a child has provided information that should be removed, please contact us and we will address it.

If you target the game to children in the store listing, additional obligations (e.g. Google Play Families policy, COPPA) may apply — configure ad content and audience settings accordingly.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Decline or reset personalized ads via the in-game consent choice and your device settings.
  • Remove local data by uninstalling the game.
  • Request access to or deletion of leaderboard data associated with you by contacting us at the email above; because leaderboard identity is anonymous, please include your nickname.

10. Security

We rely on the security measures of Google and Unity for data they process. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect information.

11. International transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than yours. Their transfers are governed by their respective privacy policies.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date at the top of this page.

13. Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact ahaaygames@gmail.com.
